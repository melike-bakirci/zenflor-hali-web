# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS

## Users

Both B2B clients (Architects, Contractors, Businesses) and B2C retail customers looking for flooring solutions.

## Product Purpose

A digital storefront and informational site for a company selling carpet tiles (karo halı) and artificial grass (çim halı).

## Positioning

Premium quality and durability.

## Operating Context

Visitors browsing flooring solutions for commercial or residential spaces, comparing options, and seeking contact information or references.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing routing structure includes products (Karo Halı, Çim Halı), blog, references, contact, and legal pages.
- Site is built with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
- Multi-language support capability (i18next installed).

## Brand Commitments

None binding. Open to new ideas without strict pre-existing constraints on brand assets.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains an incumbent visual implementation and content structure, including routing and basic pages.

## Product Principles

- **Quality-First Representation**: The digital experience must reflect the premium quality and durability of the physical products.
- **Dual Audience Clarity**: Navigation and content should cater clearly to both business/contractor needs and direct retail consumers.
- **Frictionless Discovery**: Users should easily find product details, references, and contact paths without unnecessary complexity.

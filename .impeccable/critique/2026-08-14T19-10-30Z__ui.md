#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The site functions, but it looks like a generic AI-generated template. It leans heavily on "safe" but overused tropes that rob it of brand identity—specifically, it has the standard "Inter font + thick side border" aesthetic that feels machine-made rather than authored for a premium flooring company.

**Deterministic scan**: The detector confirmed this, flagging 10 issues. The most critical are:
- **Overused font (Inter)** throughout the app (`index.css`, `LanguageSwitcher.css`).
- **Gradient text** on metrics or headers (`index.css`), a classic decorative AI tell.
- **Side-tab accent borders** (thick left/bottom borders on cards and sections) found in `About.css`, `BlogDetail.css`, and `Legal.css`.
- **Layout property animation** (`width` transition in `Home.css`) which causes layout thrash.

#### Overall Impression
The structure works for a product catalog, but the visual execution feels cheap and templated due to AI default patterns. Removing the side borders, dropping the gradients, and picking a distinctive font will instantly elevate it to the "premium" positioning the brand wants.

#### What's Working
- Clear catalog structure (Products, Blog, References) handles the dual B2B/B2C audience well.
- Responsive foundation with Tailwind.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Generic AI Aesthetic**: The use of Inter and thick side-tab borders makes the site feel like a generic template rather than a premium flooring brand.
  - **Why it matters**: Erodes trust in the "premium quality" brand commitment.
  - **Fix**: Replace Inter with a distinct sans-serif or elegant serif. Remove all `border-left` and pseudo-element side borders on cards.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset` or `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Janky Layout Animations**: Animating `width` on the homepage.
  - **Why it matters**: Causes layout thrash and feels cheap/broken during interaction.
  - **Fix**: Change transition to use `transform: scaleX` or opacity.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize` or `/impeccable animate`

- **[P2] Gradient Text**: Gradient text used for decorative purposes rather than meaning.
  - **Why it matters**: A common AI tell that detracts from a clean, premium look.
  - **Fix**: Replace with solid brand colors (like `--color-primary`).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: The layout thrashing from the `width` animations on the homepage could be particularly jarring on slower mobile devices, making the site feel unpolished and hard to tap accurately.

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Might not notice the side borders consciously, but the overall "gradient + generic font" feel might subconsciously signal a lower-tier company rather than a premium flooring provider.
